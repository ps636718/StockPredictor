Rolling Normalization Rationale

Stock price time series are notoriously non-stationary. By scaling Close, High, Low, and Open against a 252-day rolling mean (100-base), the model isolates relative cyclical momentum while eliminating long-term price level drift.

Multi-Horizon XGBoost Architecture

Rather than recursive single-step autoregression (which compounds errors), we deploy 7 dedicated Gradient Boosted Decision Tree models, individually tuned for each forecast horizon using Optuna hyperparameter optimization.

Sequential LSTM Recurrent Network

Trained on sliding 10-day historical sequence windows with dropout regularization (0.2) and Adam optimization, capturing temporal dependencies and inertia across non-linear market shocks.
